pub fn detect_service_stop_avset(events: &[EvtxEvent]) -> Vec<EvtxDetection>
Expand description

Detect a cluster of net stop <svc> or sc.exe stop <svc> executions where ≥3 targets are in the canonical AV/backup service list within 60s.

Single service stops have medium FP risk. A cluster of ≥3 canonical services stopped within RANSOMWARE_KILL_WINDOW_NS is near-zero-FP (T1489 — Service Stop; T1562.001 — Impair Defenses).
