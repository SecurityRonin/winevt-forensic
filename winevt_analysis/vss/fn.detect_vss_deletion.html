Expand description

Detect VSS shadow copy deletion events.

Fires on Application channel EID 8193 (VSS service error — fired when vssadmin delete shadows or wmic shadowcopy delete runs) and EID 524 (snapshot deleted). QWCrypt deletes shadow copies to prevent recovery of encrypted Hyper-V VHD/VHDX files (T1490).

Returns one detection per matching event.
