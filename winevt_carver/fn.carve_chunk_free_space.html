pub fn carve_chunk_free_space(
    chunk_data: &[u8],
    chunk_offset: u64,
) -> Vec<FreeSpaceCarvedRecord>
Expand description

Scan the free-space region of an EVTX chunk for deleted records.

chunk_data must be the full 65 536-byte chunk starting at ElfChnk magic. chunk_offset is the byte offset of the chunk in the source file.

The live-record area (0x200..free_space_offset) is skipped; only bytes from free_space_offset onward are searched for record magic (0x2A 0x2A 0x00 0x00).
